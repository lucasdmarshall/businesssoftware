## Application Usage Notes

- These are default role titles and should be configurable per organization.
- A user may have one primary role and, where permitted, additional functional roles.
- Department-level RBAC should control dashboards, records, actions, approvals, and reports.
- Organization-level roles such as Owner, CEO, and authorized executive roles may receive cross-department access according to explicit permissions.
- The role hierarchy and permission tree should be separate concepts: a person's seniority does not automatically grant access to every system feature.
