# Octopilot Business OS — Development Roadmap

## Phase 1: Platform Foundation

- Organization setup
- Authentication and MFA foundation
- Users, departments, teams, and reporting lines
- RBAC and data scopes
- Audit logs
- Notifications
- File attachments
- Basic offline sync

## Phase 2: Shared Work Management

- Tasks
- Projects
- Team views
- Recurring work
- Calendar
- Scheduling
- Comments and mentions
- Custom groups and fields

## Phase 3: HR Operations

- Employee profiles
- Attendance
- Leave management
- Onboarding and offboarding
- HR approvals
- Organization chart

## Phase 4: Finance Operations

- Expenses
- Invoices
- Budgets
- Purchase requests
- Vendor records
- Multi-level approvals
- Finance reports

## Phase 5: Sales, CRM, IT, and Operations

- Leads and customers
- Sales pipeline
- IT tickets
- Assets
- Access requests
- Procurement
- Inventory
- Branch operations

## Phase 6: Intelligence and Ecosystem

- Executive command center
- Custom report builder
- KPI framework
- Risk alerts
- Scheduled reporting
- API platform
- Webhooks
- External integrations

## Product Strategy

Build the platform core before building many department dashboards. The quality of identity, data isolation, workflows, auditability, and synchronization will determine whether the company can safely operate on the system.
